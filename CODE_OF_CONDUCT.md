# Code of conduct

Ghost follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks,
sexualised language or imagery, and publishing someone's private information
are not acceptable anywhere in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other channel the project uses.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **info@northtek.io** with "Ghost conduct" in
the subject. Reports are read by the maintainers, handled promptly, and kept
confidential. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits,
code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may
restrict or ban a participant, following the enforcement guidelines in the
Covenant.
